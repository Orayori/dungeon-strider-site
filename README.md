## 🗡️ About the Game

**Dungeon Strider** is an offline-first step-counter RPG for Android where the real world is your engine.

Walk to power your hero. Every step you take becomes **Step Coins (SC)**, and every coin brings you closer to your next upgrade: a stronger weapon, tougher armor, a deeper dungeon floor. Your phone does the counting, so you can put it in your pocket and just live your day.

Your adventure doesn't pause when you do. Go for a walk, run errands, take the long way home, then open the app and see how much stronger you've become.

> **No grinding at a desk.** Your progress comes from moving, not tapping.

## 💚 Why I Built This

I made Dungeon Strider because I wanted us to be more active and healthier, and I wanted it to be fun instead of a chore.

Most games ask you to sit still. This one asks you to get up. A walk around the block, a stroll after dinner, taking the stairs: all of it becomes power for your hero. The game is just the reason to start. The real reward is the energy, the fresh air and the healthier habits you build along the way.

So if you've ever struggled to stay motivated to move, let your hero do the nagging. Every step you take is good for you *and* good for your character.

## ✨ Features

| Feature | Description |
|:--|:--|
| 🪽 **Walk to Earn** | Turn real-world steps into Step Coins, even for steps your watch uploads later. |
| 🪙 **Step Coins** | Earn currency through movement and spend it on upgrades, revives and more. |
| ⚔️ **Gear Progression** | Collect equipment and build a stronger character, one upgrade at a time. |
| 🔥 **50 Evolution Tiers** | Evolve every piece of gear through 50 tiers, from humble beginnings to legendary power. |
| 🃏 **Card Sockets** | Monsters drop cards that slot into your gear, from common to mythic. They survive every upgrade. |
| 🏰 **Dungeon Exploration** | Descend floor by floor and face increasingly difficult monsters. |
| 👹 **Boss Floors** | Every 10th floor hides a tougher boss with bigger rewards. |
| 🎽 **Six Gear Slots** | Equip head, body, legs, shoes, weapon and wings, all shown on your pixel character. |
| 🛡️ **Arena PvP** | Duel other players' saved builds and climb the ranked ladder, even while they're offline. *(In testing.)* |
| 🏆 **Leaderboards** | Compete with friends on total, daily, weekly and level boards. |
| 💀 **Revive System** | Fall in battle? Pay SC to get back up, or wait for a free revive. |
| 🔊 **Sound Effects** | Hits, crits and rewards all have audio cues, with a one-tap mute. |
| 📱 **Offline-First** | The whole game plays without internet. Only the leaderboards and arena go online. |

## 🎮 Core Gameplay Loop

<p align="center">
  <b>WALK</b> → <b>EARN SC</b> → <b>UPGRADE</b> → <b>EXPLORE</b> → <b>REPEAT</b>
</p>

1. **Walk in the real world** and your steps are counted for you.
2. **Earn Step Coins** from every step you take.
3. **Upgrade and evolve your gear** to raise your attack, defense and health.
4. **Enter the dungeon** and fight your way down, floor by floor.
5. **Collect cards and loot**, socket them into your gear, and grow stronger.

## 🏆 Walk Farther Than Your Friends

Steps are the great equalizer. No one can buy their way to the top of the board; the only way up is to get out and move. Invite your friends and family, compare your totals, and cheer each other on. A little friendly competition makes it easier to stay active together.

## 🛠️ Development

Dungeon Strider is a personal game-development project that blends real-world activity, RPG progression and retro-inspired pixel art into a mobile game.

### Project Goals

- Help people move more and build healthier daily habits.
- Build a satisfying step-based progression system.
- Make every upgrade and reward feel meaningful.
- Deliver an immersive pixel-art dungeon RPG experience.
- Keep the game accessible offline and comfortable on mobile.

## 🚧 Project Status

Dungeon Strider is under active development. Features, balance and the overall experience may change as the game grows.
