When sending feedback

  • ◆Never send your Google password, authentication codes, API keys, or database credentials.
  • ◆Don't attach private health data. A step-counting game doesn't need screenshots of your unrelated health information.
  • ◆If your issue concerns online leaderboard privacy, include only your public username unless more information is requested securely.
