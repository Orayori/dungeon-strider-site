Adventurer Milestones

A collection of challenge ideas for walkers and dungeon explorers. Use them as personal goals or community prompts while the game’s tracked achievement system is still separate.

Important: These are suggested milestones for fun—not achievements currently recorded, unlocked or verified by the Android game. The website does not read player saves.

Challenge ideas

👟

First Stride

Take your first meaningful walk while using Dungeon Strider.

Walking challenge
🪙

Gear Fund

Save enough Step Coins to buy or upgrade a piece of equipment without spending them as soon as you earn them.

Resource challenge
🧰

Full Loadout

Work toward equipping all six available slots: head, body, legs, shoes, weapon and wings.

Equipment challenge
🗺️

Into the Depths

Reach a new personal deepest floor, then prepare before taking on the next one.

Exploration challenge
👑

Boss Breaker

Prepare for a boss floor, which appears every tenth floor under the current rules.

Combat challenge
🪽

Tier Climber

Pick one equipment slot and follow its progression through higher tiers over time.

Long-term challenge
