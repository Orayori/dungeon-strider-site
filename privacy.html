Privacy Policy

Dungeon Strider is an RPG (Role-Playing Game). This page explains what information the game uses and what happens to it.

What information is used

What stays on your phone

Your game progress (gears, cards, level, currency and settings) is stored on your device. If you allow it, the game reads your step count from Health Connect on Android to power your character.

The game does not upload your raw health data. Only the step totals described below are sent to the leaderboard.

What we collect when you sign in

If you choose to sign in with Google to join the leaderboards, we receive and store:

  • Your Google account identifier, and the email address and name Google provides. These are used only to identify your account and are never shown to other players.
  • The public username you choose. This is shown on the leaderboards.
  • Your step totals (total, weekly and daily), your level, and the time of your last update, so the leaderboards can rank you and detect impossible totals.

You can use the game fully offline without signing in.

How we use it

To run the leaderboards, keep your profile tied to your account across devices, and prevent cheating. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Sign-in is handled by Google. Account and leaderboard data is stored with Supabase, our database provider. These services handle data under their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and leaderboard profile, email orayori@outlook.com from the address linked to your Google account, or include your public username. We will delete your profile and associated data.

Data stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions or data requests? Email orayori@outlook.com.

For a data deletion request, include your public username or write from the email address linked to your Google account so we can identify the relevant profile.
