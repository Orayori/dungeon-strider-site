Dungeon Strider

A step-counting dungeon RPG.

Your real world steps power your character. Walk to earn Step Coin (in-game currency), spend them on gear and upgrades, collect cards, and descend the dungeon's floors.

You can step away for days, and when you log back in, a massive stack of Step Coins will be waiting for you, tracked from every single step you took in the real world while you were gone. Grab your coins, upgrade your gears, and get right back to conquering deep dungeons and testing your strength against other players.

Plays offline: The game keeps your progress on your phone, so you can play without a connection.

Leaderboards: Sign in with Google to join the online leaderboards under a public username you choose. Your Google name and email are never shown to other players.
