Dungeon Strider

A step-counting dungeon RPG.

Fuel your competitive edge by transforming your real-world steps into valuable Step Coins to purchase elite gear and conquer deep dungeons, ruthlessly dominating rival players with a massive stack of currency that accumulates automatically from every single step you take while offline. Invite your friends to download the app today and see who can claim the leaderboard by turning their daily steps into ultimate victory.

Plays offline: The game keeps your progress on your phone, so you can play without a connection.

Leaderboards: Sign in with Google to join the online leaderboards under a public username you choose. Your Google name and email are never shown to other players.
