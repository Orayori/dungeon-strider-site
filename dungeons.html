How scaling works

Normal encounters

Monster HP, ATK and DEF gain configured increments per floor after floor 1. The actual monster is selected from the data-driven roster based on its allowed floor range.

steps below floor = max(0, floor − 1)
HP = base HP + HP growth × steps
ATK = base ATK + ATK growth × steps
DEF = base DEF + DEF growth × steps

Boss encounters

Boss floors occur on each configured multiple of ten. The current settings apply a 2× monster stat multiplier and a 4× reward multiplier to boss floors.

boss floor: floor % 10 = 0
boss stat multiplier: 2×
boss reward multiplier: 4×
The calculator reads the versioned settings bundled with this website. Your device's saved deepest-floor progress affects replay rewards, so the displayed replay estimate cannot know a particular player's actual save.
