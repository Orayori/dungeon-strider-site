How to Play

Your everyday movement powers a small offline-first RPG. Walk to earn Step Coins, strengthen your gear, and push deeper into the dungeon one fight at a time.

The gameplay loop

👟

1. Walk to earn SC

Your phone's supported step-count source supplies steps. The current settings use a one-to-one Step Coin conversion, subject to the game's validation and reward limits.

🪙

2. Spend wisely

Step Coins are the spendable currency. Use them to buy or upgrade equipment and pay other in-game costs. Spending SC does not reduce your lifetime step total.

🛡️

3. Improve your build

Equipment adds attack, defense and health. Six equipment slots can evolve through the current 50 tiers; the item catalog lists the actual stats and prices.

⚔️

4. Descend floor by floor

Enter the dungeon to fight monsters. Monster stats and SC rewards scale with the floor. You can retreat after a floor and keep earned rewards.

👑

5. Prepare for bosses

Boss floors are scheduled at multiples of ten. Bosses use stronger stats and a larger reward multiplier, so upgrade your equipment before pushing deeper.

📴

6. Play offline

Your main game progress is stored locally so you can play without a connection. Google sign-in is optional and is used for the game's online leaderboard feature.

Leveling and progression

Lifetime steps drive level

Your level is derived from your lifetime step total. Spending your SC doesn't erase your accumulated steps or directly lower your level.

The game’s current tuning settings define level progression and base stat growth.

Equipment makes the difference

Compare an item’s rarity, tier, price and ATK / DEF / HP bonuses in the Item Codex. Different slots add up to your character’s overall stats.

Before you start a long run

  • ◆Keep your phone's step permission enabled so the game can read supported step data.
  • ◆Upgrade multiple equipment slots instead of only one slot when it improves your overall stats.
  • ◆Bosses arrive on every tenth floor; check the Dungeon Guide to preview the current scaling rules.
  • ◆Back up or preserve your device if you care about local progress. Online leaderboard scores are not a cloud save of your full inventory.
This guide reflects the current repository data and settings. Some roadmap concepts may not yet exist as tracked in-game features; those are labeled clearly on the Milestones page.
